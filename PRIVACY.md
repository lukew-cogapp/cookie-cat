# Privacy policy for Cat vs Bugs

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Cat vs Bugs collects nothing.

The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no way to type anything into it. It
makes no network requests. There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash
reporting, no in-app purchase and no third-party SDK of any kind.

It asks for one Android permission, `WAKE_LOCK`, which stops the screen dimming
during a run. It grants no access to anything on the device.

## What the game stores, and where

One file, holding how many cookies you have earned, which cats, hats and places
are unlocked, and whether the sound is on. It is written to the app's own
private storage on your device, and on the web version to the browser's
`localStorage`.

Nothing in it leaves the device, because there is nowhere for it to go. It is
not a profile: it is a score and a list of unlocked hats.

Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's data for the web version, deletes
it. There is nothing else to delete, and nothing for us to delete on your
behalf.

## Children

The game is made for children, and this is why it is built the way it is: with
nothing to collect, nothing to buy and nobody to talk to, there is no personal
information for a child to give away.

## Distribution

Google Play records its own download and crash statistics for every app on the
store. That is Google's collection rather than the game's, and it is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
